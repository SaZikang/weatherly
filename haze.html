Know what you are breathing

Haze, PM2.5 and PM10

01

What is haze?

Haze is a suspension of fine particles and smoke that reduces visibility and can travel hundreds of kilometres across regions.

02

Why particle size matters

PM10 includes dust and smoke particles up to 10 micrometres. PM2.5 is smaller and can reach deep into the lungs, making it the bigger health concern.

03

Common causes

In Malaysia, haze is often linked to peat and forest fires, agricultural burning, traffic, industry, and smoke carried in by seasonal winds.

About this data and the official service

Weatherly uses Open-Meteo's modelled air-quality data because the official DOE APIMS/MyEQMS endpoints are not exposed for direct use by this static GitHub Pages site. Values are estimates and should not replace official DOE, MOH, or local authority advisories.
